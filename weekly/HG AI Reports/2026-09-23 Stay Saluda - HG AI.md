## On putting more into ToF
1. ToF has already been scaled. Giveaway spend went from $8–12/day (24 Aug–16 Sep) to about $25–27/day on 18–22 Sep. Keyword Engagement last delivered 17 Sep. The playbook caps budget changes at 20% every 3–4 days; let this settle before the next step.
2. The Giveaway campaign won't help the retargeting pool quickly: giveaway campaigns send people to the Instagram profile and Facebook page, not the website. It grows engagers, the MoF campaign turns them into site visitors, and only then do they reach BoF, so the effect is delayed.

## Recommendations, in order
1. Fix the retargeting pool directly, as the playbook's frequency rule says: fresh ad creative volume (4–6 active ads), review-led creative in the format being tested for BoF, widen the BoF audience with the approved additions (Instagram and Facebook engagers from the last 365 days, and the GHL lead list). Works faster than ToF spend.
2. Keep scaling ToF in steps of 20% or less, judged on ToF measures (cost per profile visit, followers gained per dollar, engagers converting to leads in MoF), not ROAS. Switching Keyword Engagement back on, pull its DMs, emails captured and promo-code uses; having no Meta purchase value isn't a reason to kill it.
3. Point creative at the open calendar: next 30 days 47% occupied with 222 nights left to sell; next 90 days 25% occupied with 949 nights. NC Saluda Mtn New #6 has nothing booked in the next 30 days; Good Times Cabin at 20%. Feature under-booked units before touching rates. The account has no prospecting campaign running, so that's worth a conversation, though using the shared guest list as a seed needs the contract permission in place first.
