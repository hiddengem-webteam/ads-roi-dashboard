## What the winners share

1. **Five of the top six are the six-beat template the paid Myrtle Beach reel uses.** Title card with occasion and location over a dusk or aerial marina shot, rooftop, one add-on scene (private chef in 1, 3, 5, 6; massage in 6; fire pit in 2, 3), interiors with a "for you and 3 best friends" or "retreat" line, and a "Comment 'FLO' for $100 off" close. Every scene captioned. 14 to 17 seconds. The organic winners and the paid winner are the same creative.
2. **Girls-trip framing wins on engagement, couples framing wins on reach.** Reels 1, 3, 5 and 6 are girls-trip titles and carry the saves and comments (reel 3: 139 saves, 277 shares, 218 comments, the most engaged reel in the window). Reel 2 (couples, Pasadena) matched them on views with a third of the comments.
3. **Shares are the signal that separates the top from the rest.** Top six share rate 2.60% of views versus 1.08% for the other 33; save rate 1.34% versus 0.69%. People send these reels to the friend they want to go with, which is exactly the primary-text hook the paid reel uses ("The group chat has been talking about this trip for months").
4. **Reel 4 is the outlier worth copying as a hook.** One caption for the whole 11 seconds ("apparently the first person that pops up when you hit 'share' is who you want to spend the weekend with at this luxury houseboat"), couples footage, no close card. 104 shares, 20 saves, 6 comments: pure share-bait, and it reached as far as the template reels. A cheap second hook for the Potomac reels.

## What the losers share

- **The bottom six are all September and October "Comment FLO" reels at 1,100 to 1,800 views**, the same template with the same caption. Median views by month: July 6,345, August 3,690, September 2,373, October 2,051. The template is wearing out on the organic audience, or distribution has shifted; either way the same six beats posted twice a week no longer clear the median. Reel 2 (14 October, a new location and a couples angle) is the exception, which points at fatigue rather than reach loss.
- **The caption change did not help.** The 25 reels with the "Comment FLO" caption have a median of 2,671 views; the 13 older "Booking Details" captions have 3,431. Comment-to-DM mechanics add comments, not views.

## Recommendations

1. **Keep the six-beat template for the paid reels; rotate it on organic.** The paid National Harbor reel already matches the organic winners beat for beat. On organic, the same template is fatiguing: alternate it with the reel-4 share hook and with single-scene posts so the template reels land fresh.
2. **Add the share hook as a second paid variant.** "The first person that pops up when you hit share is who you're taking" over the rooftop toast, with the VIP100 close kept. Cheapest new hook with evidence behind it.
3. **Lead girls-trip creative with the chef scene.** Reels 1, 3, 5 and 6 all carry it and it is the one scene the couples reel lacks. The paid reel has it.
4. **Music pool.** Three house or dance tracks at a similar tempo behind the top three reels. If Ads Manager rejects them, the cleared fallback is a Sound Collection track in the same mood, swapped with the script. Log the outcome in the ledger either way.
5. **Rerun weekly.** The pull takes about a minute per client; next clients named in the creative review: Stay on 30a, Tàberg Falls, Paradise Pointe, The Cohost Company.
